Water Eject provides low-frequency tones, a tone generator, sound-level estimates, photo/video capture and stereo channel checks. This policy explains how the app uses data and permissions on your device.

Audio processing on your device

Cleaning tones, the tone generator and stereo tests generate sound on your device without uploading audio.

With microphone permission, ordinary sound-level measurement processes microphone levels live without saving a recording or sending sound to a developer server. The camera uses the microphone for live readings. Only videos you actively choose to record include an audio track. Still photos do not contain an audio recording.

Camera captures, temporary files and sharing

Camera and microphone access is used when you enter and use the camera feature, for its preview, readings and capture. Photos and videos are created on your device. Exported pictures include sound-level estimates and time.

The app keeps exports in its temporary directory so the system share sheet can read them. It removes the corresponding temporary files after normal sharing completion or closing the camera. An unexpected exit may leave files until later system cleanup. Copies you save to Photos, Files or another service are not deleted with the app's temporary files.

The app does not automatically upload captures. When you select a sharing destination, that destination handles the content under its own rules. Content saved to Photos may also sync according to your iCloud Photos settings.

Local preferences and progress

Language, appearance, onboarding status and completion dates for regular cleaning sessions are stored in the app's data on your device. The app does not require registration or sign-in. Local preferences and cleaning progress are not synced through a developer service; subscription data processing is explained below. System backups or device migration may preserve local data according to your Apple device settings.

Accounts, cost and tracking

Water Eject offers Apple auto-renewable subscriptions without registration or sign-in. Prices, billing periods, eligible introductory offers or free trials, and subsequent renewal prices are shown before purchase and confirmed by Apple. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the App Store. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs.

To verify subscriptions, prevent duplicate accounting, and restore access, the app sends a random anonymous member identifier stored in Keychain, Apple-signed transactions, product and subscription-chain identifiers, and the transaction environment to the developer-operated subscription service. The service stores subscription status, offer-redemption records, and your refund-data preference. Basic network request information may be processed for service operation and security. Apple processes payments; we do not receive card details or upload audio, photos, or videos to this service. With your explicit permission, we may share subscription information, such as subscription duration and trial use, with Apple when you request a refund. Permission is off by default and can be withdrawn in Settings. Deleting the app may not remove Keychain entries or server transaction records. Contact compass_ai@163.com for access, correction, or deletion requests; necessary transaction records may be retained for legal and financial obligations.

App downloads, system backups and other Apple system services may operate according to your device settings. Apple's own processing is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions and control

You can revoke microphone or camera permission in system Settings. The related measurement or capture feature will then be unavailable; tone playback and stereo checks remain usable. System saving functions are used only when you choose to save through the share menu.

Stopping a measurement, closing the camera or moving the app to the background stops the relevant audio/capture operation. Preferences can be changed in Settings. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data. System backups and exported copies must be managed separately in the relevant system or service.

Support website and GitHub Pages hosting

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit its pages, GitHub may process your IP address, request time, requested page, and basic request information sent by your browser to provide, secure, and operate its service. GitHub handles this hosting-related data under its Privacy Statement.

This website includes no advertising, third-party analytics scripts, or information-submission forms. The Water Eject app itself does not collect these website access logs or send them to the developer. Information you choose to email to support is used to respond to and resolve your request.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email compass_ai@163.com or use the App Support destination on the App Store product page. Share only the information needed to resolve your request. This policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated if a future version introduces new data processing.
