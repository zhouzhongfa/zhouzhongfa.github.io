OfficeFlow (displayed as word文档手机版 in Simplified Chinese) is provided by Zhou Zhong Fa for creating, editing, managing, recognizing, scanning, printing, and converting documents. This policy explains the information processed, its purposes, recipients, and your choices.

No registration is required. Documents are stored on your device by default. Only files you submit for online conversion are uploaded. After you accept the terms and the Privacy Policy, Umeng basic analytics is enabled automatically for users with a confirmed mainland China region, with no additional checkbox; it is not initialized and does not report data for overseas or unconfirmed regions. Apple ad attribution synchronizes automatically when you open the app. The app does not display third-party ads, sell personal information, or track you across apps.

1. Documents and online conversion

Imported, created, and generated files are stored in the app's Documents directory. Apple Vision OCR, scanning, PDF merging, splitting, compression, encryption, and image/PDF tools run on your device and do not upload document content.

When you select a file and start online conversion, the selected file, its name, the target format, and necessary technical information are sent over HTTPS to our backend and forwarded to CloudConvert. Our backend deletes its temporary copy after forwarding. CloudConvert removes task files and results according to its retention policy. Online services may process information outside your country, including through our backend in China and CloudConvert's infrastructure. Review the upload notice and avoid submitting highly sensitive information, trade secrets, or material subject to transfer restrictions. You can use on-device tools without uploading files.

2. Subscriptions and restoration

Apple handles App Store payments; we do not receive payment card details. The app verifies StoreKit-signed transactions on your device. Purchases, restoration, renewals, expiry, and revocation snapshots are synchronized with our subscription backend using Apple-signed credentials, product and transaction information, and a random member UUID. This supports verification, restoration, renewal records, support, and subscription conversion analysis.

Signed credentials can contain transaction and original transaction identifiers, product, price and currency, purchase/expiry/revocation dates, trial type, and environment. The random UUID is stored in Keychain/local settings and is passed to Apple as appAccountToken. We do not require your name, telephone number, or email address. Records are separated by app; another app's subscription does not unlock OfficeFlow. Association is restored using Apple-signed transactions, rather than a transaction number alone. Deleting the app does not cancel renewal, and Keychain identifiers may remain.

Information sharing for refund review and withdrawal

The first-use dialog explains refund-review information sharing and links to the relevant terms and this policy. By tapping Agree and Continue to accept this version of the terms containing that provision, you agree that, only when you request an in-app purchase refund, we may provide Apple with necessary purchase and trial information, entitlement delivery status, and our refund preference to assist its review. This sharing does not include document content or require payment card details. Apple decides whether to approve a refund and its amount; permission does not guarantee an outcome or limit your statutory rights.

The app records the version of the terms actually accepted, the acceptance time, and the permission status on your device. Your acceptance or refusal for refund sharing is saved with your random member identifier by our subscription service to verify permission and handle withdrawal. The corresponding choice is updated only after you actually accept terms containing this provision or actively change the setting. Acceptance of an older version is not automatically converted to new permission, and an existing explicit refusal or withdrawal is not automatically overwritten. If synchronization fails, a pending record is retained for retry when a connection is available; unconfirmed permission is not treated as already saved by the service.

You may withdraw permission at any time by turning off “Allow usage information to be shared with Apple when requesting a refund” in Profile → Privacy and Security, or turn it on again in the same place. This permission can be withdrawn separately and does not control basic analytics or Apple ad attribution. Declining or withdrawing does not affect document features, purchased subscription access, or restoration. After withdrawal, we no longer submit new refund-review information on the basis of that permission; prior lawful processing is unaffected. If saving fails, the app displays a message; check that your change has been saved successfully. For information already submitted to Apple, you may also request access or deletion through Apple's Data and Privacy page.

Umeng basic analytics

After you accept the terms and the Privacy Policy, Umeng is initialized automatically only with a confirmed mainland China region, based on the mainland China App Store storefront (CHN) or the member's previously confirmed domestic region. Umeng is not initialized and no data is reported to it for overseas users or while the region is unconfirmed. Reporting success, failure, or timeout does not affect startup, documents, subscriptions, or navigation. When enabled, Umeng measures installations, active users, and sessions. It processes SDK-generated identifiers such as UMID, ZID, and IDFV; device model; operating system and app version; launch/session times; and necessary network information such as IP addresses. Automatic page analytics and entry collection are disabled. We do not add custom button, purchase, or page events, send document content or member profiles to Umeng, integrate advertising identifier modules, or read IDFA, contacts, or precise location.

Apple Ads / AdServices

Apple ad attribution status synchronizes automatically with our backend when the app opens or returns to the foreground. No manual switch is required. This is independent of the choice in the terms dialog and whether Umeng is enabled or its reporting result. When an Apple lookup is needed, an attribution token is obtained from Apple and sent only to Apple's official attribution endpoint, not to Umeng. Apple's result may include attributed status, organization/campaign/ad group/keyword identifiers, country or region, conversion type, and available names or click dates. The result is uploaded over HTTPS to our backend with the same random member UUID and a random installation authentication token to measure Apple ad performance and subscription conversion. Until a valid Apple result is available, our backend records an unknown source. Network/token failures are not treated as organic installs. Later valid responses may fill missing fields for the same source. This is not used for personalized advertising across apps.

5. Permissions and local storage

Camera access is requested for user-initiated scanning. Photo access is limited to photos you select or authorize. The system file picker accesses files you choose to import, open, or save. Denying a permission affects only the relevant feature; permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Preferences, random identifiers, and necessary caches use system storage. You can manage, export, back up, or delete local documents. Uninstalling normally removes sandbox files but does not cancel subscriptions.

6. Retention and security

Local documents remain until you delete them. Conversion files are removed as described above and under the provider's policy. Subscription, member association, and attribution records support ongoing verification, renewals, support, analytics, and auditing, and are handled when their purpose ends or we receive a legally valid deletion request. Records subject to legal retention duties may be retained. Umeng data follows its service policy and deletion process; you can also use Umeng's deletion request page. We limit retention to the necessary period and use HTTPS, access controls, and protected server configuration. Service secrets are not bundled in the app.
