Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026 · Version: 2026-11-17

Welcome to AI Compass Assistant. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following information to provide our services:

• Location: with permission, coordinates and altitude support weather, maps and activity recording. • Sensors: accelerometer, magnetometer, gyroscope and barometer readings support the compass, level and altimeter. Sensor readings are processed locally. • Device information: device model, OS and app version may be used for compatibility, troubleshooting and analytics. • Subscriptions: Apple processes payments. We store transaction identifiers, signed purchase information and an installation member ID for reporting and reconciliation; we do not receive payment-card information.

2. How we use information

We use this information for the following purposes:

• Provide weather, coordinates, maps, compass, level and altitude features. • Understand app usage, improve features and fix problems. • Adapt the app to your preferences. • Maintain service reliability, prevent fraud and protect security.

3. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

• Umeng analytics (iOS): device and usage information helps us understand and improve the app. Policy: https://www.umeng.com/page/policy. • Apple Ads attribution: advertising attribution is associated with an installation identifier for campaign reporting. • QWeather: rounded coordinates are sent to the weather provider for local weather and sunrise/sunset data. • Maps and geocoding: Apple Maps on iOS and AMap on Android process map and location requests under their own privacy policies. • Apple In-App Purchase: Apple manages payments and subscriptions. We do not receive payment-card details.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

• With your explicit consent. • When required by law or a valid government request. • With the service providers listed above, as necessary to provide those features and subject to their privacy policies. • In a merger, acquisition or business transfer, with notice where required.

Refund review information sharing and withdrawal

The agreement prompt explains refund review information sharing and links to the Terms of Use and this policy. By tapping Agree and Continue to accept this version containing this clause, you agree that, only when you request an in-app purchase refund, we may provide Apple with necessary purchase and usage information related to that purchase, together with a refund handling recommendation, to assist its review. Limited to relevant information we actually hold, this may include time since installation, app usage time, an anonymous member identifier, whether the purchase was used and access was delivered, trial status, purchase amounts and previous refund amounts. This sharing does not include photo content, precise location or activity routes, and does not require payment-card details. Apple decides whether to approve a refund and its amount under its policies. Consent does not guarantee a refund outcome or limit your right to request a refund or other statutory consumer rights.

The app records the accepted agreement version, time and language on your device. Your refund sharing choice is stored with a random member identifier by our subscription service to verify your choice and process withdrawal. Acceptance of an older agreement does not automatically grant this consent, and accepting updated terms does not override an explicit refusal or withdrawal. Failed synchronizations remain pending for retry; an unconfirmed choice is not treated as saved by the service.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off Information Sharing in Settings, or enable it again there. Refusing or withdrawing does not affect compass features, purchased subscription access or Restore Purchases. This choice does not control analytics or Apple Ads attribution. After withdrawal, no new refund review information is submitted based on this consent; earlier lawful processing is unaffected. If a change has not synchronized, check its status and retry on that page. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of relevant records.

5. Storage and security

• Storage: activity tracks, photos and sensor readings are stored locally. Weather, map and geocoding requests send necessary location information to their providers. Subscription and attribution reports are sent to our backend and may be processed outside your country. • Retention: local records remain until deleted or the app is removed. Necessary transaction records are retained for reconciliation and legal obligations; providers follow their own retention policies. • Security: we use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted network transport and access controls.

6. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

• Access, correct or delete information through the app or by contacting us. • Revoke location, motion, camera and photo permissions in device settings; related features may become unavailable. • Cancel automatic renewal in your App Store subscription settings. • No registration is required. Removing the app deletes its local app data; exported photos remain in your library. Contact us about backend records or other rights available under applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. Guardians who believe a child has provided information may contact us to request deletion.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app, and consent will be requested where required. Please review this policy periodically.
