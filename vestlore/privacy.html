Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: November 1, 2026 · Vestlore 1.0

English简体中文繁體中文

Vestlore is operated by Zhou Zhong Fa ("we", "us"). This policy covers the iPhone app, this support website and support correspondence. Contact: compass_ai@163.com.

Version 1.0 automatically creates a guest account. Learning, preferences and virtual portfolio records are stored by our backend for your installation. No Apple sign-in, advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchase or subscription is offered in this version.

1. Data used to provide the app

We process a randomly generated installation identifier and secret, guest user identifier, session credentials, app language, learning goals and duration, onboarding status, sound/haptic preferences, lesson and lab attempts, answers, completion records and XP. We also process your virtual cash, holdings, simulated order previews and confirmations, and portfolio history. These are educational records, not real brokerage accounts or real financial holdings. The app does not request your name, email, Apple identity, brokerage login, payment details, IDFA or precise location.

These identifiers and records are linked to your guest account and used for app functionality: maintaining progress, retrieving content, running virtual practice, securing access, and handling data export or deletion. They are not used for cross-app tracking or advertising.

2. Network services and local storage

The app communicates with our backend at trade.zzfwzx.xyz over HTTPS. Market information is retrieved through that service; company or instrument images may be downloaded from the HTTPS source URLs supplied by the service. Network providers can receive technical request information such as an IP address to deliver and secure those requests. Market data may be delayed or cached.

Installation secrets and session credentials are stored in iOS Keychain. Content, preferences, account data and pending learning submissions may also be cached on the device. Some saved lessons can be read offline; online features require a connection. Guest access depends on the installation credentials. Version 1.0 does not provide cross-device sign-in or guaranteed recovery after credentials are lost. Removing the app alone does not request deletion of server records, and Keychain data may persist after removal.

3. Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to answer and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, installation secrets, session tokens, real financial information or other unnecessary sensitive data. Contacting support does not automatically send a data export. Optional support messages are handled at your request and by our email provider.

4. Sharing, hosting and international processing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Necessary service providers process information to operate backend hosting, network delivery and email. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect lawful rights and safety. Providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable law and their policies.

This website uses GitHub Pages; GitHub may process IP addresses and technical access logs under its privacy statement. We add no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts to these pages. Apple separately handles App Store, system backups and any diagnostics you choose to share under its privacy policy. External educational websites have their own policies.

5. Retention, export and deletion

Guest account records are retained to provide the service until you request deletion, subject to processing time and any information that must be retained for security, dispute handling or legal obligations. In the app, open Profile → Settings → Export my data to request a copy, or Delete account to request deletion of the guest account and associated learning and virtual portfolio records. Deletion requires confirmation and cannot be undone. The app shows the server’s deletion status. Resetting virtual practice is separate from account deletion and does not delete learning records.

Support messages are retained only as reasonably needed to manage the request or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion or other applicable privacy rights. We may need proportionate verification. You may contact the relevant data protection authority where the law provides that right.

6. Security and legal grounds

We use HTTPS, Keychain credential storage and access controls to help protect data; no electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Where a legal basis is required, we process service data to provide the requested app functionality, pursue legitimate interests in service security and support, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Children, updates and contact

Vestlore is general financial education and is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable local minimum age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us for review and appropriate deletion. We update this policy and its date when practices change. Privacy questions or requests: Zhou Zhong Fa, compass_ai@163.com.
