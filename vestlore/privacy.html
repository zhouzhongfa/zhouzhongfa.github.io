Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 21, 2026 · Applies to Vestlore 1.0

English简体中文

Vestlore is operated by Zhou Zhong Fa ("we," "us"). This policy explains how the Vestlore iPhone app, this support website, and optional support correspondence handle information. Contact: compass_ai@163.com.

The app keeps learning and virtual portfolio data on your device. Version 1.0 has no account system, developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK, and does not send your learning or portfolio records to us.

1. Information stored in the app

The app stores your selected learning goal and duration, onboarding status, completed lessons and labs, XP, and virtual portfolio information (cash, holdings, and virtual transactions) in local app storage. This enables progress and practice to continue between sessions. These are educational records, not a real brokerage account or real financial holdings. The app does not ask you to provide a name, email address, brokerage credentials, or payment details.

2. Optional support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach, such as a screenshot or device/app version. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and manage the support request, not for advertising. Where a lawful basis is required, we process correspondence at your request and on our legitimate interests in providing secure support, or to meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, brokerage login information, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive data. We do not automatically receive your local learning records when you contact us.

3. Website, Apple services, and other providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs, to operate and secure hosting under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising, analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts to these Vestlore pages. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store support correspondence.

Apple may separately process App Store, device backup, or diagnostic information under its policies and your settings. System backups may include local app data; Vestlore does not provide its own cloud sync. See Apple's privacy information. External educational links, if opened, are governed by those websites' policies.

4. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-app advertising or tracking. Support correspondence is handled through the providers needed to deliver and manage email. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect lawful rights and safety. Website hosting and email services may process information outside your country; applicable privacy protections and provider policies apply. Local app progress is not uploaded by us for these purposes.

5. Retention, reset, and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you reset them or remove the app. Profile → Reset demo progress resets learning and virtual transactions to the initial state and returns to onboarding; it writes default initial values rather than deleting a server account. Deleting the app through iOS removes its current local app data; separately retained system backups are managed through Apple's settings.

We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed to handle and maintain the request or comply with legal obligations. You may ask us to delete support emails; some information may need to be retained where legally required. We cannot remotely inspect or erase local app records that were never sent to us.

6. Your choices and security

You may reset local data in the app, remove the app, or choose not to email support. Where applicable law provides access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other privacy rights regarding information we hold, contact us at the address below. You may also contact the relevant data protection authority where the law provides that right. We may need proportionate verification before acting. We take reasonable measures to protect support correspondence, but no electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

Vestlore is general financial education, not a service directed to children under 13 or another minimum age required locally. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if our practices change and revise the date above. New features involving accounts, online services, payments, or data collection require an updated disclosure before those practices apply. For privacy questions or requests, contact Zhou Zhong Fa at compass_ai@163.com.

隐私政策

生效与更新日期：2026年10月21日 · 适用版本：Vestlore 1.0

Vestlore 由 Zhou Zhong Fa 运营（以下简称“我们”）。本政策说明iPhone App、本支持网站及自愿发送的支持邮件如何处理信息。联系方式：compass_ai@163.com。

学习与虚拟组合记录保存在你的设备。1.0版没有账号系统、开发者运营的后端、广告SDK或统计SDK，不会向我们上传学习或组合记录。

1. App中的本地信息

App在本地保存学习目标与时长、引导完成状态、已完成课程与情景、经验值，以及虚拟现金、持仓和交易，以便下次继续使用。这些是教学记录，不是真实券商账户或资产。App不要求提供姓名、邮箱、券商登录凭据或支付资料。

2. 自愿发送的支持邮件

如果你主动发邮件，我们会收到邮箱地址、正文及你自愿附上的截图、设备或版本信息，用于回复、排查和处理支持请求，不用于广告。在法律要求处理依据时，我们基于你的请求、提供安全支持的合法利益或适用法律义务处理邮件。请不要提供密码、券商凭据、支付资料或其他不必要的敏感信息。联系支持不会自动向我们发送本地学习记录。

3. 网站、Apple服务与服务提供方

本网站由GitHub Pages托管。GitHub可能为托管运行与安全处理IP地址、访问日志等请求信息，详见GitHub隐私声明。我们没有为这些Vestlore页面添加广告、统计、Cookie或追踪脚本。邮件服务商会为投递与保存支持邮件而处理相关信息。

Apple可能按其政策和你的设置处理App Store、系统备份或诊断信息。系统备份可能包含本地App数据；Vestlore本身不提供云同步。详见Apple隐私信息。如打开外部教育来源链接，适用对应网站的政策。

4. 共享与跨境处理

我们不出售个人信息，不为跨App广告或追踪共享个人信息。支持邮件由投递与处理所需的服务提供方处理。依法必须披露，或为保护合法权利与安全所必需时，我们可能披露相关信息。托管和邮件服务可能在你所在国家/地区以外处理信息，适用相关隐私保障及服务商政策；我们不会为这些目的上传本地学习记录。

5. 留存、重置与删除

本地记录保留到你重置或删除App。在“个人/进度”页面选择“重置演示进度”，会把学习与虚拟交易恢复为初始状态并返回引导；操作会写入默认初始值，并不是删除服务器账号。通过iOS删除App会移除当前安装的本地数据；系统另外保留的备份按Apple设置管理。

支持邮件仅在处理、维护请求或履行法律义务所合理需要的时间内保留。你可以请求删除邮件，但依法必须保留的部分可能除外。对从未上传给我们的本地记录，我们无法远程查看或删除。

6. 你的选择与安全

你可以重置本地记录、删除App或不发送邮件。在适用法律赋予访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对、可携带等权利时，可通过下方邮箱提出请求；法律允许时，也可向相关数据保护机构投诉。处理前可能需要适度核实身份。我们采取合理措施保护支持邮件，但任何电子存储或传输都无法保证绝对安全。

7. 未成年人

Vestlore面向一般金融知识学习者，并非专门面向13岁以下或当地规定最低年龄以下儿童的服务。我们不会明知而要求儿童提供个人信息。如认为儿童已向支持邮箱发送个人信息，请联系我们核查并酌情删除。

8. 更新与联系

信息处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期。未来如增加账号、在线服务、支付或数据收集，会在相关处理开始前更新披露。隐私问题与请求请联系 Zhou Zhong Fa：compass_ai@163.com。
