Contacting support

Describe the issue and steps to reproduce it. You may include your app/iOS version, iPhone model and a screenshot with private details removed. Never send passwords, installation secrets, session tokens, real financial balances or brokerage credentials.

Does the app trade real money?

No. Balances and orders are virtual. There is no brokerage connection, deposit, withdrawal or real-money order. Market information is real-world data where available, but may be delayed or cached. Simulated execution can differ from actual markets.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Version 1.0 automatically starts with a guest account. The backend saves learning and virtual portfolio records for your installation. Cross-device sign-in and recovery are unavailable; do not rely on reinstalling to recover records.

Where can I export or delete data?

Open Profile → Settings → Export my data or Delete account. Account deletion removes associated learning and virtual portfolio records after server processing and cannot be undone. My assets → Practice rules → Reset virtual practice resets the virtual portfolio only. Removing the app alone does not request server deletion.

Why is content or a quote unavailable?

Lessons may unlock as you progress. Online content and market features need a connection; try again if the service or a quote is temporarily unavailable. The app does not invent prices when source data is missing. A market instrument may be viewable even when virtual practice is unavailable for it.

Language, appearance and purchases

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese follow your iPhone language or per-app language setting. Appearance follows the system. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in version 1.0.

Support covers app usage, not personalised investment advice. See our privacy policy.
