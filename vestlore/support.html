Describe what happened and what you expected. If helpful, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot with personal information removed. Do not send passwords, real account balances, brokerage credentials, or payment information. We cannot see your local progress unless you choose to share it.

Frequently asked questions

Does Vestlore trade real stocks?

No. All transactions use virtual cash and fixed educational prices. There is no broker connection, deposit, withdrawal, real-money order, or live market feed.

Why are some lessons locked?

Lessons unlock sequentially as you complete the previous lesson. This is a learning path, not a payment lock. Decision labs are accessible from Learn.

How do I change the language or theme?

English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese follow your iPhone's preferred language or per-app language setting. Depending on your iOS version, check Settings → Apps → Vestlore → Language, or Settings → General → Language & Region. Appearance follows Settings → Display & Brightness.

Where is progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

Progress and the virtual portfolio are saved on the current device. Version 1.0 has no developer-operated account or cloud sync. Apple system backups are separate and depend on your settings; we cannot retrieve local records for you.

How do I start over?

In Profile, select Reset demo progress and confirm. This resets learning and virtual transactions to the initial state and returns to onboarding. Use this only when you want to restart; the app does not provide an undo or cloud restore service.

Can I buy a subscription or restore purchases?

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Included lessons and practice do not require a purchase. There are no purchases to restore in this version.

Are the scenarios exact historical backtests?

No. They are simplified or normalized educational reconstructions inspired by market history. Illustrative charts and virtual results do not predict actual investment returns.

We can help with the app, but cannot provide personalized investment advice or manage a brokerage account. See the privacy policy for support email handling.

这是真实股票交易App吗？

不是。仅使用虚拟资金与固定教学价格，没有券商连接、入金、出金、真实订单或实时行情。

为什么课程没有全部解锁？

课程按完成顺序逐节解锁，并不是付费限制。情景实验可从学习页面进入。

如何切换语言与主题？

支持英文、简体中文与繁体中文，跟随iPhone首选语言或App语言。按iOS版本，在“设置 → App → Vestlore → 语言”或“设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区”中调整。主题跟随“设置 → 显示与亮度”的浅色/深色外观。

进度能跨设备同步吗？

1.0版只在当前设备保存学习与虚拟组合，没有开发者提供的账号或云同步。Apple系统备份另依设置管理，我们无法代为找回本地记录。

如何重新开始？

在个人/进度页面选择“重置演示进度”并确认。课程与虚拟交易将恢复初始状态并回到引导。请仅在确定重新开始时操作；App不提供撤销或云端恢复服务。

有订阅或恢复购买吗？

1.0版没有App内购买或订阅，内置课程与练习无需购买。因此该版本没有需要恢复的购买项目。

情景是否使用精确历史行情？

不是。情景是受市场历史启发的简化或归一化教学重构，图表和虚拟结果不能代表真实投资回报。
